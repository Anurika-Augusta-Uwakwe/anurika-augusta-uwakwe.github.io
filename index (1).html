Sample 2 · Ranking justification

Pairwise A/B comparison

Response AvsResponse B · preferred

Response B is better. Both answers address the question, but Response A states a percentage that does not appear in the source, which is an unsupported claim. Response B stays within the source, uses exactly three sentences and keeps a neutral tone. Response A is slightly more fluent, but a hallucinated figure outweighs style.

Preference: B, strongly. Deciding factor: factual accuracy.

Sample 3 · Research task

Multi-step question with a reference answer

Question

A Nigerian federal university, now one of the country's best-known research institutions, began in 1948 as a university college with a special relationship to a foreign university. Identify the institution's original name, the university whose degrees its first students earned, and the year it became an independent degree-awarding university under its current name. Rule out the University of Nigeria, Nsukka, which opened in 1960 and is sometimes mistaken for the first Nigerian university.

Reference answer

University College, Ibadan. Its students first earned University of London degrees. It became the independent University of Ibadan in 1962.

Every real submission also records a supporting quotation, the source URL and the date I verified it.
Sample 4 · Hallucination check

Checking each claim against the source

Source excerpt. The clinic opened in 2019 and serves about 400 patients a month.
"The clinic opened in 2019."Supported
"It serves about 400 patients a month."Supported
"A national health charity founded it."Unsupported
